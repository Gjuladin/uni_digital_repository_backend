# Repository rights operations

The configured deposit licence grants UIST a non-exclusive distribution and
preservation licence. It is not a substitute for verifying the depositor's
rights. Before operating this repository publicly, UIST must maintain these
controls:

1. Require a depositor to accept the deposit licence and record the acceptance
   with the submission, account, date, and licence version.
2. Require a rights statement or licence for each public item and preserve it
   in metadata. Do not treat a Creative Commons label as proof that the
   depositor owns the underlying rights.
3. Verify publisher, funder, co-author, privacy, confidentiality, and embargo
   restrictions before publishing material.
4. Publish a rights-complaint/takedown contact and process. On a credible
   claim, restrict public access while UIST reviews it; preserve the audit
   record and communicate the outcome to the depositor and claimant.
5. Have UIST's authorized legal representative approve the deposit licence,
   rights policy, privacy policy, and takedown process before production use.

The final published policies must identify UIST's exact legal entity, contact
details, governing law, and review/escalation process. Those organisation-
specific terms are intentionally not guessed in source code.
